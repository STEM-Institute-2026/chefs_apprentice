# Chef's Apprentice App Overview (some features are still in development)

* This is a  learning based app meant to target teenagers and help them learn how to cook in a quick and fun way. 
* This app has a catalog of thousands of different healthy recipes with the recipes ranging from an easy difficulty to an expert difficulty. 
* You can get in contact with chefs who have uploaded their recipes on this app to gain tips and learn cooking skills that you can use for different recipes. You will be able to upload pictures of the final result of the recipe you have made with the help of our app’s step by step guide. You will also be able to rate each recipe that you make which will be shared to others to see whether the recipe is good or not. 
* Each recipe you complete you earn points which the user earns to level up and unlock the next difficulty with more advanced recipes and skills that could help the user increase their cooking confidence. 
* You will be able to search for a specific type of recipe as well as save recipes to your favorites which you can either make again later or share with your friends. 
* You will also be able to shop for the ingredients meant for the recipe through our app. Our app will be supplied by local supermarkets and you will be able to purchase the necessary ingredients.
